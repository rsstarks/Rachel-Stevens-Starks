## Research Interests

My research interests focus on vertebrate paleontology, dinosaur diversity, and expanding our understanding of Wyoming's prehistoric ecosystems. I am particularly interested in identifying gaps in Wyoming's dinosaur fossil record and investigating underrepresented or poorly documented dinosaur groups within the state.

I also have a strong interest in hadrosaur dinosaurs and their importance to the fossil record. I am interested in exploring how their fossils can provide valuable information about dinosaur diversity, distribution, and the environments they inhabited.

### Research Themes

- **Wyoming Dinosaur Diversity:** Identifying gaps in the state's dinosaur fossil record and documenting underrepresented taxa.

- **Hadrosaur Paleontology:** Exploring the diversity, distribution, and scientific importance of hadrosaurs.

- **Cretaceous Paleobiology:** Using dinosaur fossils to better understand ancient ecosystems and biodiversity.

- **Wyoming's Paleontological Heritage:** Expanding knowledge of Wyoming's fossil resources and encouraging their preservation.

## Current Research

### Wyoming Hadrosaurs

My research explores how hadrosaur dinosaurs can help us understand the health of Cretaceous ecosystems. By studying these dinosaurs, I hope to contribute to a better understanding of Wyoming's ancient environments and paleontological history.

## Keeping Wyoming's Fossils in Wyoming

As a Wyoming native, I hope to work alongside local communities, ranchers, and landowners to help preserve our state's fossil heritage and keep Wyoming's paleontological resources connected to the people and communities where they are found.

I want to help build paleontology programs here at home that expand access to education, inspire curiosity about our ancient past, and create opportunities for students to explore Wyoming in a new way.
